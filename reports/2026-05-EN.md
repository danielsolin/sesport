# sesport – May 2026

sesport began taking shape during the final days of May. The main focus was
defining what counts as Swedish participation and how people, teams,
countries, and other entities should be connected.

The data model was normalized early and the PostgreSQL structure was
simplified. Tools for normalizing entity names were also added, laying the
groundwork for combining information from different sources.

At the same time, the first AI experiments started: local activity search,
LM Studio integration, and tests with LLM-based web search.

**In short:** May established sesport's core idea, data model, and first
AI-based search flows.
