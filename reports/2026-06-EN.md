# sesport – June 2026

During June, sesport moved from basic structure to a much more complete
workflow. Several parts that later became central now started working together
in practice.

## Key changes

- **The activity workflow became clearer.** Activities gained better admin
  support, multiple related entities, and clearer filters for day-to-day work.
- **Sports broadcasts became a central data source.** They were connected to
  activities, TV channels, and entities.
- **Importing became more systematic.** Rules for hiding or ignoring
  irrelevant broadcasts reduced the amount of manual cleanup.
- **The AI layer became its own system.** Configuration, jobs, tool calls, and
  detailed run logging were brought into a more coherent workflow.
- **AI runs became more resilient.** Asynchronous execution and restart
  handling made them much more useful in continuous operation.

The public presentation was refined at the same time, including denser activity
lists and better links between activities and participants.

**In short:** June was the month when sesport started behaving like one
coherent working tool where broadcast data, activities, entities, and AI worked
together.
