# sesport – July 2026

During July, much of the focus shifted to participants and to making AI results
more useful in day-to-day work. Participant data became richer, more traceable,
and clearer in the public presentation.

## Key changes

- **Name matching became more robust.** Validation became more forgiving when
  spelling and formatting varied.
- **Each participant gained its own evidence.** Supporting material could be
  linked directly to the participant, making information easier to verify and
  follow up.
- **Participant data became richer.** Status, facts, and start times became
  part of the activity flow and appeared more clearly in the public interface.
- **Participant lists became more useful.** Sorting, mobile layouts, and the
  presentation of relevant information were improved.
- **AI jobs became more specialized.** Facts, participants, and results could
  be handled separately, with outputs stored and applied directly to
  activities.
- **Automation moved forward.** Configurable AI workflows began shifting work
  from manual runs toward increasingly self-running processes.

Activities and broadcasts also started reusing participant data more
effectively, reducing duplicate work and making the overall model more coherent.

**In short:** July made participants first-class data in sesport and took a
major step toward a more automated, evidence-based workflow.
