# Sweden-Relevant Sports Entity Watchlist for SE Sport

## Inclusion Criteria

I treated a candidate as a good **step-one watchlist entity** if it is Swedish by identity, ownership, representation, or place-based significance, and if it is stable enough to keep generating future international sport-related activity without being rebuilt every week. That means I favored Swedish national teams, Swedish elite athletes, Swedish clubs with recurring international relevance, Swedish recurring events, and Swedish governing bodies that reliably generate discoverable evidence. I did **not** treat foreign clubs or foreign teams as stable Sweden-relevant entities merely because a Swedish athlete plays there; in those cases, the Swedish person is the root entity and the foreign club is a relationship target. High-confidence inclusions below are grounded mainly in official federation roster pages, official tour/player profiles, official club/competition pages, and official event sites.

## Source Gaps and Open Questions

The areas where Sweden likely has important entities but **source discovery is harder** are combat sports, cycling, canoeing, rowing, curling, speedway beyond the top name, and clearly international Swedish esports. In those areas, official Swedish federation pages tend to be less standardized, athlete-level public profiles are more fragmented, or international relevance is more seasonal and harder to verify quickly from a single authoritative source. That does not mean those sports should be ignored; it means they should probably come in a second research wave with a sport-specific source strategy.  

A few current-status items also deserve explicit caution even when the evidence is official. Some 2026 club associations on current Swedish FA squad pages are surprising compared with older public expectations, but because the Swedish FA is the primary official source, I preserved those as current relationship/status claims rather than dismissing them. In implementation, I would store them with source timestamping and be prepared for rapid change during transfer windows.  

## Recommended Next Step

The best next research step is to convert this watchlist into a **normalized seed-entity registry** with four linked layers: `entity`, `current_relationship`, `evidence_source`, and `activity_template`. For each entity, I would immediately add: canonical name, entity type, sport, Sweden relevance class, priority, source registry entry, and a starter activity schema. Then, for person entities, I would separately attach foreign club/team/tour/series relationships as non-root targets; for recurring events, I would define edition activities; for organizations, I would define discovery feeds such as roster announcements, discipline/governance updates, qualification criteria, and event-host news. That will let step two collect future activity without confusing stable Sweden-root entities with volatile relationship targets.
